Dasho for Sigil

One thousand US dollars a month. Fifteen to twenty brands, up to twenty five users, held at that rate for twelve months from signing.

Sigil runs fifteen brands across four subscriptions, and leadership cannot see in one place how content is doing. This proposal covers the coordination and governance layer of that stack: every brand’s rules, drafts, approvals and history in one workspace you own.

What is not ready, said plainly

On our call we mentioned the real estate rules, including the restriction on advertising pre-selling. That rulebook is on our roadmap, not in the product today. We would rather you carry an accurate picture into your partner meeting than a flattering one.

If Sigil signs, the real estate rulebook becomes the next one we build. We will commit to a delivery date in writing before you sign anything, and it is included in your rate when it lands.

Three more things we want you to know before you decide

There is no single screen showing all fifteen brands at once. You switch between brands, and each carries its own governance score. A portfolio view across every brand is something we are building, and we are not going to describe it as though it already exists.

Metricool does reporting that Dasho does not replace. Treat this as consolidating your coordination and governance stack, not all four tools.

The governance score reports, it does not block. Today it tells you which content breaks a rule. Stopping publication on a failing score is on our roadmap.

Two questions before we go further

Your answers change what we would recommend you buy, and one of them may reduce what you need from us rather than increase it.

  1. How many of your fifteen brands are in regulated categories? Real estate, beauty, food, supplements, health or gaming. These are the brands where a single caption creates a real problem for your client.
  2. Who signs off the claims on those brands today, you or your client? If that sign-off does not formally sit with anyone, that is the most useful thing you could tell us.

How we would start

  1. Week one. We build Brand Cards with you for your five largest brands, on a call, not as homework.
  2. Week two. Your team runs live work through those five. We watch and fix what gets in the way.
  3. Week three. The remaining brands come across, and we agree which ones need a rulebook.
  4. Month two onward. A monthly review of governance scores across your portfolio, with us, for as long as it is useful.
